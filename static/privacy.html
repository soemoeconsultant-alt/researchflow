Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

ResearchFlow is a market research platform operated by Soe Moe (Research Team), Myanmar. Contact: soemoe.consultant@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

ResearchFlow accesses your Google account only after you sign in with Google and grant permission. It uses:

  • Google Drive — to store and read your project files (audio, transcripts, notes, reports) in your own Google Drive folders.
  • Google Docs — to create and update report documents in your Google Drive.
  • Google sign-in — your name and email address, only to identify you and to check that you are allowed to use the app.

Where your data lives

Your project files live in your own Google Drive. The app keeps only a temporary working copy while it runs. We do not sell, share, or rent your data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can revoke ResearchFlow’s access at any time from your Google Account security settings, under third-party access. If you have questions about your data, contact us at soemoe.consultant@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
